Privacy policy

This text is a template. Before publishing, align it with the actual business and have it reviewed by a legal adviser.

Last updated: Monday, 19 October 2026

Who processes your data

The data controller is AUTOHAUS, Novi Sad, Serbia. The workshop's contact details are listed on the Contact page.

What we collect

When you send an appointment request: your name, phone number, chosen service, preferred date and time, and optionally your email and a description of the issue. When you send a message: your name, email, optionally your phone, and the message. We never collect payment card details.

Why we use it

Only to process appointment requests, communicate about your vehicle and reply to messages. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

For as long as needed for these purposes and to meet legal obligations, after which it is deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, and to object to processing, under the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection. You may lodge a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection.

Cookies

The site uses only essential cookies: to remember your chosen language and to keep administrators signed in. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.
